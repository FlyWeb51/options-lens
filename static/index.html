Options Lens

What the options market is pricing in — and where it's stretched

Type a ticker to begin

You'll get four things:

  1. Expected move — the one standard deviation range the option market is charging for, by expiration.
  2. Implied probability distribution — the full curve of where the market prices the stock to land, recovered from the option chain, with odds for every price level.
  3. Squeeze score — a 0–100 read combining short interest, days to cover, daily short volume, dealer gamma positioning and call demand, with every component shown separately.
  4. Unusual activity — contracts where today's volume is abnormal versus what's already open.

These are risk-neutral probabilities: what the market charges for each outcome, which is not the same as what will happen. They embed a risk premium and overstate tail odds. This is a research tool, not advice.
