Eight tabs of data on any US listed company.

  1. Options — expected move, the full market-implied probability distribution, squeeze scoring, unusual activity.
  2. Volatility & Decay — the IV surface, term structure, implied versus realised volatility, and what time decay actually costs per day.
  3. Strategy — payoff diagrams with probability of profit taken from the chain's own distribution.
  4. Fundamentals — revenue, margins, cash flow and more, with year-over-year growth, charted however you like.
  5. People — officers and directors, what they're doing with their own shares, and a link to find them.
  6. Politics — lobbying spend, the issues pushed, the firms hired and who they contacted.
  7. Kalshi — real-money probabilities on rates, inflation, jobs and growth.
  8. API Stock — every data source, what it costs, and what switching it on would unlock.

Probabilities derived from option prices are risk-neutral: what the market charges for each outcome, which is not a forecast. Research tool, not advice.
